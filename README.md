# 🛡️ T&Shield — AI Legal Jargon Killer

> **Stop blindly clicking "I Agree". Understand what you're really signing in seconds.**

---

## 🚨 The Problem

Over **91% of consumers** blindly accept Terms & Conditions, rental agreements, and employment contracts without reading them. Hidden within pages of dense, predatory legalese are clauses that:

- 📊 **Sell & Monetize Private Data:** Unconsented sharing of personal browsing telemetry with third-party advertisers.
- 💳 **Silent Auto-Renewals:** Recurring credit card charges with tight cancellation windows and no refund policies.
- ⚖️ **Surrender Legal Rights:** Forced binding arbitration clauses that waive your constitutional right to trial by jury or class-action lawsuits.

---

## 💡 The Solution

**T&Shield** acts as a 24/7 AI-powered legal advisor that converts impenetrable legalese into actionable plain English in seconds.

### Key Capabilities:
1. **Dynamic Risk Score (0–100):** Visual circular risk gauge color-coded by severity (🔴 High Risk, 🟡 Moderate, 🟢 Safe).
2. **Side-by-Side Clause Breakdown:** Directly quotes predatory contract text next to its 8th-grade plain-English translation.
3. **Severity Badging:** Categorizes risks into Critical, Warning, and Info tiers.
4. **Real-World Sample Presets:** 1-click test cases for Meta/Instagram privacy terms, Spotify billing terms, residential leases, and freelance NDAs.
5. **One-Click Utilities:** Copy summary reports to clipboard and export clean 1-page PDF print summaries.

---

## ✨ Features Breakdown

- ⚡ **Instant Text Scanner** — High-speed analysis engine evaluating privacy, liability, and financial traps.
- 🎯 **Visual Risk Gauge** — Animated 0–100 risk rating with dynamic glow and safety labels.
- 🚩 **Categorized Clause Cards** — Original quote + Plain English translation + "Why It Matters" consequence.
- 🔍 **Severity Filter Tabs** — Interactively filter clauses by Critical, Warning, or Info levels.
- 📱 **Real-World Presets** — Instant testing using actual terms from Instagram, Spotify, and Rental Contracts.
- 📋 **Executive Summary Export** — Copy quick summary or print a formatted 1-page PDF report.
- 📱 **100% Mobile Responsive** — Modern dark-mode layout designed for mobile, tablet, and desktop viewports.
